Good to know

Questions, answered simply.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the WhatsApp or call link on this page. Appointment availability must be confirmed directly by the clinic.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Ask the clinic when booking. They may request relevant dental or medical information and a list of current medicines.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Ask the clinic for a written estimate after assessment. Fees and coverage vary by provider and treatment.

Can I ask about treatment options?

Yes. A qualified dental professional can explain suitable options, benefits, risks, and expected follow-up for your situation.

What if I have a dental emergency?

For severe pain, swelling or bleeding, contact a dental clinic promptly or your local emergency number if urgent help is needed.

Is this a real dental clinic?
